## Natural hazards

Ratings come from FEMA's National Risk Index (FEMA, National Risk Index, December 2025 release): 3 = High (FEMA "Very High" or "Relatively High"), 2 = Moderate ("Relatively Moderate"), 1 = Low (everything lower, including hazards FEMA does not expect here). They compare each county with every other county in the country, and they weigh how much there is to damage as well as how often the hazard strikes, so a thinly settled county can rate Low for a hazard that still happens there. ## NOAA Weather Radio

A weather radio receives these transmitters with no power grid or internet. Program the SAME code of every county you care about so the radio sounds only for warnings there; reception is reliable to about 40 miles over flat ground and less in hills (NOAA National Weather Service, NOAA Weather Radio Coverage by County, 2026). → 15-01
