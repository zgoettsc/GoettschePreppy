# Water filters and purifiers: what they remove, and common models

**What this page is for.** Portable water filters differ in the one thing that matters most:
which germs they stop. This page explains the words on the box, then lists common filters with
what each maker says it removes and how much water it is rated for. How to make water safe, step
by step: → 03-04. Looking after a filter, including freezing: → 03-09. The words micron, bacteria,
protozoa and virus: → 29-06.

## What matters and why

1. **What it removes, by germ type, in the maker's own words.**
   - **Bacteria** (for example *E. coli*, cholera).
   - **Protozoa**, also called parasites or cysts (*Giardia*, *Cryptosporidium*).
   - **Viruses**, which are far smaller than bacteria.
   **If the box does not say viruses, assume it does not remove them**, and disinfect the water
   after filtering (chlorine or boiling, → 03-04, → 29-05). Makers call a device that is rated
   for viruses a **purifier**.
2. **How sure: the percentage or "log" figure.** 99.9999 % is "6 log": one germ in a million gets
   through. More nines is better. A maker may also name a test protocol (NSF P231, NSF P248).
3. **Pore size, in microns.** A micron is a thousandth of a millimetre. In the table, 0.1 to
   0.2 micron filters are rated for bacteria and protozoa; the two 0.02 micron purifiers are also
   rated for viruses.
4. **Rated capacity.** How much water before the filter element is worn out, "depending on water
   quality". Dirty water wears it out far sooner. Count what goes through it.
5. **Flow rate.** Litres per minute or per hour. A family needs a gravity or pump filter, not a
   drinking straw.
6. **What it does not do.** None of these remove dissolved chemicals, fuel, salt or most metals
   unless the maker says so; some say plainly they do not (→ 03-03, → 03-07).
7. **Freezing.** A hollow-fiber filter that has frozen wet may be cracked inside with nothing to
   see. **If a used filter has been below freezing, or you cannot be sure, retire it** (→ 03-09).
8. **"For clear water only."** Some makers say this. Let muddy water settle or pre-filter it
   through cloth first (→ 03-04).
