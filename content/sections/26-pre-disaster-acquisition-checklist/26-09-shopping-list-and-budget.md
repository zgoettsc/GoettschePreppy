# The result: ranked shopping list and budget

**What this page is for.** The walk is not finished until this page is filled in. Everything
before it was diagnosis; this is the prescription. **Do not shop the same day you walk**: sleep
on it, then buy in the order below, which is → 22-07's order: *what hurts people soonest, then
what cannot be bought later.* Typical rows for this household are already printed in each tier,
from your settings; add your own ✗ and ~ items in the blank rows.

## Do this now

1. **Move every ✗ and every ~ from the five checklists** into the right tier below.
2. **Anything from → 26-03 (life safety and medical) goes in Tier 0**, whatever it costs and
   whatever else is on the list.
3. **Set a monthly amount you can actually sustain** and work down. Steady beats a spree,
   because the ⌛ items need years, not money.
4. **Put the ⌛ items on the calendar now**, even if you cannot buy them yet.

## The ordering rule

Three questions, in order:

1. **How fast does its absence hurt someone here?** Minutes, hours, days, weeks.
2. **Can it still be bought later?** ⌛ items (seed, seasoned wood, soil, children's next sizes,
   skill) **cannot**. They move up regardless.
3. **How likely is the event it covers?** A multi-day power cut is near-certain over a decade
   (American Red Cross, Power Outage Safety){{#if is.cold_winters}}, and here it will come in
   winter sooner or later (American Red Cross, Winter Storm Safety){{/if}}. Buy against the likely
   first, then the severe.

**Two marks worth carrying over:**

- **§ = has a consumable.** Buy the spare in the same order: the element, the lids, the file, the
  batteries. Forgetting it is the most common preparedness mistake.
- **⌛ = time-sensitive.** Money cannot fix it later.

## When the shops close

This page becomes read-only.

- **The list is good until the next walk**, about a year.
- **If the budget is small,** buy **Tier 0 complete, then the ⌛ items in Tier 2**, and let Tier 1
  wait. Cheap-and-unbuyable-later beats expensive-and-still-available.
- **Re-walk yearly** (→ 25-10) and re-rank from what you find.
- **Tier 4** (skills, practice, neighbours, a destination) is free and outranks most of what is
  above it.
