# Checklist 5: children, elders, pets, records and morale

**What this page is for.** The last sheet of the walk (→ 26-01): the months items, and the
cheapest. Documents cost almost nothing and cannot be replaced if lost (USA.gov, Replace Your
Vital Documents; IRS, Reconstructing Records After a Disaster). Morale supplies look frivolous on
a list and are why people keep functioning in month three (→ 21-02).

**Marks:** ✔ have it, it works, I know where it is · ~ partly, or needs a spare · ✗ do not have

> → 21-02). Budget for morale the way you budget for food.
