# Checklist 4: sanitation, clothing, tools, security and comms

**What this page is for.** The fourth sheet of the walk (→ 26-01): the weeks-to-months items.
Nothing here kills you tonight; all of it decides whether month three is manageable or
miserable. **Sanitation is first on purpose**: a sanitation failure turns into a medical
emergency fast, especially with small children or elders in the house (→ 13-11).

**Marks:** ✔ have it, it works, I know where it is · ~ partly, or needs a spare · ✗ do not have
