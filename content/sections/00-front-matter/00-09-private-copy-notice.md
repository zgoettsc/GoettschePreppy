# Your private copy: what it holds and why you must not share it

**What this page is for.** It says what happened to the answers you typed, what is inside this
file, and why the file is for your household only. It is the same notice you saw on the settings
page and on the download screen, printed here so it travels with the binder.

## Your answers are never stored

Nothing you typed on the settings page was saved by the people who run it. There is no account,
no database and no log of your answers. Your answers lived only in your browser tab while you
filled them in. If you chose to save a copy of your answers as a file on your own device (a
`profile.json` file), that file is yours and stays with you.

When you pressed **Generate**, your answers were sent once to the AI service that writes the
binder (Anthropic's Claude API), through a small relay program. The relay's only jobs are to
check your access key and pass the request along. It keeps a count of requests and nothing else.
Its source code is shown on the settings site so you can read what it does. Cloudflare, which
hosts the settings page and the relay, keeps its own standard request records (times, sizes,
rough location) like any web host; nobody has asked it to keep anything more. The AI provider's
own data-handling terms apply to that request; the people who run this kit do not control those
terms, and say so here rather than promise more than is true.

**Photos you add to the binder never leave your device.** They are attached in your browser and
saved into a new copy of the file only when you press "Save a new copy".

**Your saved answers file is private too.** It holds your household's names, ages, medical
conditions, medicines and plans. Keep it where you keep other private papers.

## This file is your private copy. Do not share it.

{{#if size.large}}
{{#if size.everything}}**You downloaded the Everything binder.** It contains the full text of every
document this binder cites{{else}}**You downloaded the Large binder.** It contains the full text of
the documents cited by the life-safety sections (medical conditions, water, food storage and
canning, fire and fuel, heating, power, first aid, hazards, children, radiation and chemical
events, decision cards and floors){{/if}}: appliance manuals, first-aid guides, fire-safety
standards, medical-society fact sheets, university publications, written by other people and
organisations. They were fetched for **your personal use** when you downloaded this file,
because in an emergency you need the whole document in your hand, not a web address that no
longer works.

Those documents are copyrighted by their publishers. Giving your binder file to someone else
copies their work without permission and breaks copyright. **Keep this file to your own
household.**
{{else}}
**You downloaded the Small binder.** It holds the kit's own text, your household pages, and
public-domain material such as the plant plates and the Air Force survival handbook in Section
31. No source document is bound inside it; each one is named by title, publisher and date on the
page that cites it.

**Keep it to your household anyway.** It is full of your family's private details: names, ages,
medical conditions, medicines, meeting places and plans. And if you later upgrade to Large or
Everything, that copy will contain other people's copyrighted documents fetched for your
personal use only, and sharing it would break copyright.
{{/if}}

**If a friend wants a binder,** do not send them yours. Ask the person who gave you your access
key to give them their own, and they can make a binder for their own household. Theirs will
carry their own region, their own medicines and their own choices, which yours does not.

## What this binder is, and is not

This binder was assembled and checked by an AI from a verified core text, public datasets and the
answers you gave. **No doctor, engineer, electrician or other professional has reviewed your
copy.** It is a reference for a time when the normal references, services and professionals are
not available. While they are available, use them. Where this binder and a professional disagree,
the professional is right.

Several pages ask you to choose between options that carry real risk. Those pages explain the
hazard, what the manufacturer or agency says and why, what reduces the risk, and the choices. The
choice is yours and so is the responsibility for it. The full statement is → 00-05.
