# Phase-transition checklists (A → B → C)

**What this page is for.** Do-not-think checklists for the moments when thinking is hard: the
event starting, deciding to leave, loading up, arriving, and coming home. Every line is explained
elsewhere. **Do not stop to read the explanation now; follow the list.** Print this page and keep
it at the front of the paper copy (→ 00-10).

> **CAUTION.** **Never run a generator, charcoal, or any engine indoors, in a garage, or near an
> open window.** **A tested CO alarm goes up before any fuel-burning heater is lit.** **A
> frozen-then-thawed insulin vial or pen is discarded.** (→ 30-01)

## → Entering Phase A (the event starts)

- [ ] Everyone accounted for: {{ household.labels }}. No fire, no injuries, no CO (→ 08-02)
- [ ] **Write down the date and time**
- [ ] **Refrigerated medicine at a safe temperature; rescue medicine at room temperature**
      (→ 29-07). If the outage looks long, move refrigerated medicine into a powered cooler or
      cooling pouch, **checked with a thermometer first** (→ 09-16, → 12-21)
- [ ] **Powered medical devices on battery; spare batteries charging** (→ 09-06)
- [ ] **Fill every water container**: tub, jugs, pots, bottles (→ 03-02)
- [ ] **Charge everything to full while the grid flickers**: phones, power banks, medical devices{{#if has.power_station}},
      power stations{{/if}}{{#if has.solar}}; put the solar panels out{{/if}} (→ 09-16, → 00-10)
- [ ] Warm room chosen and closed off (→ 08-01)
- [ ] Wood or fuel brought under cover, enough for several days (→ 06-03)
- [ ] **CO alarms installed and TESTED** in the warm room at pillow height, **before any
      fuel-burning heater** (→ 08-02)
- [ ] Only heaters rated for indoor use. Heat while people sleep: **our choice:** {{choice DC-01}} (→ 30-02)
- [ ] NOAA Weather Radio on; find out what happened and for how long (→ 15-01)
- [ ] Sanitation plan decided before the toilet stops (→ 13-01)
- [ ] Eat the refrigerator first, then the freezer (→ 04-17)
- [ ] Count and write down: days of each medicine, days of fuel, gallons of water, days of food
      for anyone on a special diet (→ 23-02)
- [ ] Check on the neighbours (→ 18-01)

## → Considering Phase B (should we go?)

- [ ] Has a **trigger** from → 01-02 actually been met? Which one: ______________
- [ ] **Where exactly** are we going? ______________ What is its status? ______________ (→ 01-05)
- [ ] **Route and alternate**, and is it survivable right now? (→ 01-03)
- [ ] **What happens on arrival**: shelter, heat, water on day one? (→ 01-04)
- [ ] **Is it genuinely better than staying?**
- [ ] Weather checked: **do not depart into a storm**; stay off the roads in a winter storm if at
      all possible (Ready.gov, Winter Weather; → 15-01)
- [ ] Daylight for the whole trip
- [ ] Enough of every medicine and medical supply for the trip **and** a delay (→ 01-07)
- [ ] Every adult agrees
- [ ] **If any box is unchecked: STAY, and fix that box**

## → Executing Phase B (we are going)

**Load in this order. If you must leave early, the critical things are already in:**

- [ ] People{{#if has.pets}} and pets, carriers ready (→ 20-09){{/if}}
{{#if has.refrigerated_meds}}- [ ] **Cold chain:** powered cooler loaded and running from its battery, or cooling pouches
      ready; **thermometer shows it cold BEFORE departure**; medicine goes in only then (→ 01-07){{/if}}
- [ ] **Medicines**: every prescription, rescue medicines **on a person**, testing supplies,
      devices and chargers, printed action plans and "my meds" table (→ 01-07, → 25-09)
- [ ] **Documents and cash** grab kit (→ 19-01, → 19-02)
- [ ] Water and a way to treat more (→ 03-04)
- [ ] Sleeping bags, pads, **dry spare clothes for everyone** (→ 11-02)
- [ ] Food, including **safe food for anyone with an allergy or special diet** (→ 02-04)
- [ ] Fire, light, knife, tools (→ 06-01)
- [ ] Shelter: tent, tarps, cordage
- [ ] Radio, chargers, power banks (→ 00-10, → 09-06)
- [ ] Go-bags (→ 22-03)

**Before pulling away:**

- [ ] Vehicle fuelled (never below half a tank as a habit: American Red Cross, Power Outage
      Safety); fuel cans labelled and stowed outside the cabin unless your household
      chose otherwise (→ 30-07); winter kit aboard; **shovel and traction material** (→ 22-05)
{{#if has.trailer}}- [ ] **Trailer:** coupler latched, pin in, safety chains crossed, **all lights checked** (brake,
      turn, running), heavy items low and just forward of the axle, nothing alive or irreplaceable
      in it (→ 01-03, → 16-11){{/if}}
- [ ] Water carried inside clothing so it does not freeze
- [ ] Everyone in layers, dry, hats and gloves on (→ 11-01)
- [ ] House: water shut off and pipes drained if freezing (→ 03-06); fires **fully out**; gas off
      if appropriate (→ 00-04)
- [ ] **Note left** saying where you have gone and when (for family, not for strangers)
- [ ] Meeting point and comms plan said aloud (→ 15-03)
- [ ] The binder device charged and on a person; printed key pages aboard (→ 00-10)

## → Arriving, Phase C (first 48 hours)

- [ ] One heatable room found and warmed before dark (→ 08-01)
- [ ] **CO alarm in the sleeping space; chimney, flue or stove inspected before lighting** (→ 08-02, → 01-04)
- [ ] Medicines re-stabilised: cool but not freezing for refrigerated medicine, room temperature
      for rescue medicine (→ 29-07)
- [ ] Water source found; **treat everything until tested** (→ 03-04, → 03-07)
- [ ] Sanitation sited downhill and away from water (→ 13-07)
- [ ] Security: who else is here, what are the ways in (→ 14-01)
- [ ] Introduce yourselves to the neighbours (→ 18-01)
- [ ] Count what is on site; work out every runway again (→ 23-02)
- [ ] Routine back in place within 48 hours{{#if has.child}}, children's first (→ 20-03){{/if}} (→ 21-04)

## → Returning home (it is over, or it is safe again)

- [ ] Building checked before going in: gas smell, damage, standing water
- [ ] **Do not drink tap water until it is declared safe** (→ 03-04)
- [ ] **Throw out perishable food that was above 40 °F for more than 2 hours. Never taste to
      test** (FoodSafety.gov, Food Safety During a Power Outage; → 04-17)
- [ ] Check for burst pipes before turning the water back on (→ 03-06)
- [ ] Restock, and **work out the runways again**: you are now the household that did this
- [ ] **Update the binder while it is fresh**: what worked, what was wrong, what was missing. Write
      it on the paper copy, change your answers in settings, and regenerate (→ 00-01)
