## Drawn without a page reference yet

These figures exist in `figures/index.json` but no page lists them; the page edits are in `notes/workers/F1.md`.

- `fig-cal-hypo-two-step`: Pool shock (calcium hypochlorite), the EPA two-step method: 1, one heaping teaspoon (about 1/4 oz) of granules into 2 gallons of water makes the stock, labelled DO NOT DRINK; 2, one part stock to 100 parts water (1 pint per 12.5 gallons), stir, wait 30 minutes, expect a slight chlorine smell.
- `fig-water-heater-valves`: Getting water out of a tank water heater: heat off (gas control to OFF, or the breaker off), close the cold-water inlet valve on top, open a hot tap higher in the house, then open the drain valve at the base slowly into a hose or bucket. The water can be scalding.
