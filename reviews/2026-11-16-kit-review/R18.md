## Could not resolve

1. **Propane pounds per gallon.** I found no federal or other readable source for 4.2 to 4.24 lb/gal, so 25-02 no longer gives gallons or BTU per cylinder. A readable PERC or NIST Handbook 130 copy would bring the "20-lb cylinder ≈ 4.7 gal" figure back.
2. **Lamb's quarters and amaranth nitrate claims** ("piles up nitrate", "not from manured or drought-stressed ground") are not on the NC State page those species cite. I left them because they are cautionary. D2 or a reviewer should cite an Extension or veterinary nitrate source, or soften them.
3. **The species lists include oddities from county records.** For example, Gambel oak and Pacific yew are listed in Northern Glaciated Plains and Northwestern Great Plains (SD). The lists follow D2's "documented, not common" method, and the plate lead now says so. A botanist pass (D2 item 6) is still wanted.
4. **`cited_by` in sources/index.json** still lists the old winter keys' pages. I only added `duplicate_of`, as instructed.
5. **25-02 "Toilet flush (conventional) 1.6 to 3.5 gal"** differs slightly from 13-01 (EPA: 1.6 gal or less; older toilets 3.5 to 5). This was not in my items, so I left it.
