## Fixes (page, what, why)

1. 12-22 Table W1 heading: WHO Table 5.3 also prints the first band as "3–6 kg" (not only 6.5).
2. 12-22 §1: WHO quote had "of pharyngitis" inserted; now exact ("The only clear indication for
   antibiotic treatment is ...", note to Table 6.5).
3. 12-22 §1 duration: the 10-day course is for high-prevalence settings or a history of rheumatic
   fever "aged between 3 and 21 years" (WHO footnote c); the age limit was missing.
4. 12-22 §1: added WHO's statements that the second choices "should only be considered in
   patients allergic to first-choice options" and the clarithromycin resistance footnote.
5. 12-22 "How to read": WHO's no-allergy-alternatives sentence quoted exactly, with the sore
   throat exception noted (it contradicted §1 as written).
6. 12-22 §2: quote "especially in children > 2 years of age" was not in WHO; now "is appropriate
   in mild cases especially in children > 2 years" (key messages). Complications: WHO says
   imaging is needed if mastoiditis or brain abscess is suspected; "and hospital care" removed.
7. 12-22 §3: the page said antibiotics "should be considered" for symptoms over 10 days or
   worsening; WHO says bacterial sinusitis "should be suspected" in those cases and that
   antibiotics "could be considered" for severe onset, higher-risk people or red flags. Reworded.
8. 12-22 §3 IDSA Table 10: adult second line also lists doxycycline (100 mg PO bid or 200 mg PO
   qd); added.
9. 12-22 §4 CRB-65: "home treatment" and "hospital" replaced by WHO's words ("Candidate for
   outpatient treatment", "Inpatient treatment").
10. 12-22 §5: IDSA/ESCMID re-cited to the executive summary; "Amoxicillin alone is not
    recommended" replaced by the exact sentence ("Amoxicillin or ampicillin should not be used
    for empirical treatment").
11. 12-22 §5 children: "fever, vomiting or irritability (WHO)" was not WHO's list (no
    "irritability"); now WHO's sentence verbatim. CDC quote now carries "in children 2-24 months".
12. 12-22 §6: WHO Table 34.5 child ciprofloxacin is "(IV/oral)", not "(oral)"; WHO's oral-route
    note, its "no nausea and vomiting" condition and the fact that this is WHO's hospital-facility
    chapter added. Doses unchanged.
13. 12-22 §6 IDSA/ESCMID: "for patients not needing hospital" added (part of the recommendation);
    oral beta-lactam sentence now quoted exactly; ceftriaxone dose "1 g" as printed.
14. 12-22 §7 and §8: IDSA Table 2 footnote "Doses listed are not appropriate for neonates" added
    to both table captions.
15. 12-22 §7: hospital criteria now IDSA's sentence (adds poor adherence; "outpatient treatment is
    failing" rather than "failure of treatment by mouth"); "prompt surgery" corrected to "Prompt
    surgical consultation".
16. 12-22 §8: MRSA sentence completed (failed initial antibiotic treatment, markedly impaired host
    defenses, SIRS with hypotension).
17. 12-22 §9: rabies category II "or abrasions" and category III "direct contact with bats" as in
    WHO Table 18.3; bite table identified as IDSA Table 5, "adult doses".
18. 12-22 §10: "are not appropriate" was applied to toothache; WHO applies it to inflammatory
    conditions (periodontitis, irreversible pulpitis, dry socket) and says separately not to use
    antibiotics to cure toothache. Quoted exactly.
19. 12-22 §11: Yellow Book HUS warning is about children "with bloody diarrhea"; context added.
20. 12-22 §12: the "consistent with guidance published by IDSA, AAN, ACR" sentence is not on the
    erythema migrans page; it is on CDC's Clinical Care of Lyme Disease page (new source).
21. 12-22 "When antibiotics do not help" and safety box: CDC quote "Antibiotics DO NOT work on
    viruses, such as those that cause colds and flu" is not on the current page (it reads
    "Antibiotics DO NOT work on viruses." then a list); sepsis signs ("fast breathing", "fast
    heartbeat") replaced by CDC's current six signs. Same sepsis fix on 30-17.
22. 12-23: ES-600 strength "600 mg per 5 mL" → "600 mg/42.9 mg per 5 mL" (label); Flagyl 375
    storage added from the label.
