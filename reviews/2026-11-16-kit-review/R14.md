## Changes (page, what, why, source)

### Backfeed floor and the DC-07 write-up (priority)
- 09-04, 09-08, 09-13, 09-16: the backfeed floor now matches 30-01 word for word, followed by the
  cord-and-plug sentence. **Why:** the old text ("without a transfer switch, interlock, or a
  cord-and-plug conversion with the main breaker locked off") listed cord-and-plug as a way of
  connecting to house wiring. **Source:** 30-01; CPSC Pub. 5123 (backfeed endangers line workers and
  neighbours on the same transformer); Honda EU2200i manual (backfeed electrocutes utility workers;
  the generator can burn when utility power returns).
- 09-04 DC-07 write-up, rewritten as 12 steps:
  - Step 1, power off and verified dead: the appliance's own breaker off and tagged, main off as
    well if unsure, and a cabinet service switch never trusted alone. Live-dead-live meter test:
    hot to neutral, hot to ground, neutral to ground. **Source:** OSHA 29 CFR 1910.333(b)(2)(iv)(B).
  - Breaker and wire pairing: 14 AWG with 15 A, 12 AWG with 20 A. **Source:** NEC 240.4(D) as
    printed in Southwire SPEC 10028.
  - Plug ratings: NEMA 5-15 is 15 A/125 V, 5-20 is 20 A/125 V, 6-series is 250 V. **Source:**
    Hubbell NEMA chart.
  - Cord ratings: 14/3 is 15 A, 12/3 is 20 A. **Source:** Southwire SPEC 70065, NEC Table
    400.5(A)(1).
  - New: a strain-relief connector clamped on the cord jacket. New grounding step: green or bare
    wire to the green screw and the appliance's grounding point; no two-prong plug, no cut ground
    pin, no adapter. Polarity: black to brass, white to silver.
  - Receptacle box fastened and its cable clamped.
  - Start surge changed from "2 to 3 times" to 3 times running watts. **Source:** Generac GP3500iO
    manual.
  - Outage use, main breaker: kept "off, locked or taped" because fig-cord-and-plug-conversion
    shows it. The step now says this is a second habit, not the safety mechanism, so it does not
    contradict card 30-08 ("main breaker can stay as it is"). It also gives the surge reason
    (Red Cross).
- 09-04 Step 7 cords: cord rating must exceed the connected watts, three prongs, outdoor use,
  protected at doors. **Source:** CPSC 5123; OSHA QuickCard. The unsourced "close to 20 A" became
  arithmetic (1,800 W ÷ 120 = 15 A). Added Generac's "not for critical life support" for medical
  devices.
- 09-04 CO: "hundreds of cars" now cites `cpsc-carbon-monoxide-information-center`, the same fix as
  30-01 in wave 2. "More than 80 deaths a year" and the 20 ft rule were re-read in CPSC 468, and the
  20 ft rule also in CDC. Generator cover: "open, canopy-like" (CPSC 5123). Refuel only when off
  and cool (CPSC 5123).
- 09-04 fuel: "stabilised roughly a year or more" changed to "months, not years", matching 29-01
  and 06-06. Added Honda's guidance (drain the carburettor if run less than monthly; put 12-month
  stabilised fuel into a car; no more than 10% ethanol). The unsourced diesel line now points to
  the manual. Unsourced kW ranges for generator types removed (→ 28-03). Honda facts added: ships
  without oil, first oil change at 20 h. The OPD-valve description was removed (unsourced); the
  requalification date stays (Flame King, → 29-14).

### PV voltage and cold correction
- 09-02, 09-05: ×1.20 is now tied to sources.
  - Panel makers' Voc coefficients: −0.28 %/°C (Renogy RSP200D) and −0.25 %/°C (EcoFlow 400W).
  - NEC Table 690.7: 1.20 holds down to −13 °F and rises to 1.25 at −40 °F. Read in Mike Holt's
    printing of the table.
  - New rule: use 1.25 if the record low is below −13 °F and the coefficient is unknown.
  - Example arithmetic checked (0.003 × 65 = 0.195).
- 09-02: grid-tied systems switch off in an outage, and need an inverter plus storage to island.
  **Source:** DOE, Solar and Resilience Basics; the old "to protect line workers" was not in any
  source.
- 09-02: removed unsourced "10–15% per conversion", "MPPT +15–30%", "3–5× surge" and "grid-tie
  30–40 V". Added: "12 V" panels have a Voc of about 22–23 V (Rich Solar, Renogy); the array Voc
  must not exceed the controller rating (Victron); a 25-year lifetime (DOE; Rich Solar warranty).
- 09-02 tilt: latitude (DOE) plus solstice geometry (latitude + 23.5° faces the noon sun;
  NOAA). Latitude + 15° is stated as a common compromise.
- 09-05: day lengths changed to 8–9 h vs 15½–16 h at 45–49° N (NOAA). Cold panel gain cites Renogy
  (Pmax −0.37 %/°C).

### Series/parallel arithmetic and fusing
- Checked every worked example: 09-01, 09-02, 09-05, 09-09, 09-10 and 09-14. All arithmetic is
  right.
- 09-03: "four to six" lead-acid batteries corrected to "three to five" (600 Wh usable each).
- 09-08 wire table recomputed. Each cell is now the thinnest copper wire meeting both limits:
  - At most 2.5% drop (0.3 V), Victron's target; the old table's unsourced 3% target missed it.
    Uses the resistance values in Victron's AWG chart.
  - Within NEC copper ampacity as printed by Trojan (Table 310.15(B)(16)).
  - Cells that changed: 5 A/10 ft, 20 A at every length, 30 A/20 and 30 ft, 50 A, 100 A, 200 A.
  - Added the formula, so a reader can check other cases.
- 09-08 fusing re-sourced to Victron Wiring Unlimited:
  - The fuse protects the wire; rating is the lower of device and cable.
  - Every consumer fused after a main battery fuse; a fuse between the PV array and the
    controller.
  - DC-rated fuses only.
  - Interrupt rating in lithium systems; class T is well suited.
- 09-08: removed the unsourced "within 7 inches". It is ABYC E-11, which is not public, and Blue Sea
  returned 403.
- 09-08: "three or more parallel strings" is now checked against the panel's maximum series fuse

### LiFePO4 vs lead-acid (maker manuals)
- 09-03, 09-07, 09-13, 09-14 lead-acid figures now come from Trojan's User's Guide, Battery
  Maintenance page and FAQs:
  - Resting voltages (Table 7): flooded 12.73/12.10 V, AGM 12.84/12.24 V. "Below 11.8 V = damage"
    became "11.8 V ≈ 30% left".
  - Depth of discharge: 50% or less recommended, 80% maximum.
  - Electrolyte freezes at −92 °F when full and about 16 °F at 40% charge.
  - Life halves for every 18 °F above 77 °F; self-discharge 5–15% a month; at 0 °F about 50%
    capacity.
  - Never charge a frozen battery; no jewellery; no desulfators; never equalize AGM or gel.
- 09-07: removed the unsourced LiFePO4 voltage column and the NiMH and AGM cycle numbers.
  LiFePO4 figures now cite Battle Born: 3,000–5,000 cycles, 100% DoD, 2–3% a month, 31 lb, charge
  14.2–14.6 V.
- Cold charging (09-03, 09-05, 09-07, 09-16):
  - Station manuals give 32 °F as the lowest charging temperature (EcoFlow, Jackery, Anker), and so
    does USFA.
  - Battle Born gives 25–32 °F; its documents state both.
  - "Damage" now cites Battle Born.
  - "Lithium plating" and "permanent damage" were not sourced, so they were removed.
- Storage charge (09-03, 09-07, 09-16): "40–60%" was stated as a universal rule, but makers
  disagree. BLUETTI says 40–60%, EcoFlow 60%, Anker 100% every 3 months, Battle Born 100% and
  disconnected. Pages now say "store at your manual's level" and keep "100% before a storm".
- Lithium fire wording (09-03, 09-06, 09-07, 09-13): re-cited to USFA (Battery Fire Safety page and
  the existing USFA blog key), because the `nfpa-...lithium-io-0c7d64` archive is empty ("could not
  extract"). NFPA's warning signs, as USFA prints them, now include odd noises and colour change.
