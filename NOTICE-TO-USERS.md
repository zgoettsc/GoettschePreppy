# Notice shown inside every generated binder

This text appears (1) on the settings page while the household fills it in, (2) on the download
screen, and (3) as the second page of the generated binder, after the safety disclaimer. It is
written for someone who has never thought about any of this before.

---

## Your answers are never stored

Nothing you type on this page is saved by us. There is no account, no database and no log of your
answers. Your answers live only in this browser tab. If you close the tab before you download your
binder, they are gone and you start over. You may choose to save a copy of your answers as a file
on your own device so you can come back and regenerate later; that file stays with you.

When you press **Generate**, your answers are sent once to the AI service that writes your binder
(Anthropic's Claude API), through a small relay program whose only jobs are to check your access
key and pass the request along. The relay keeps a count of requests and nothing else. Its source
code is shown on the settings page's privacy section so you can check this. Cloudflare, which
hosts the page and the relay, keeps its own standard request metrics (times, sizes, rough location)
like any web host; it does not see your answers' meaning unless it inspected the request, and we
have not asked it to keep anything. The AI provider's own data-handling terms apply to the
request; we do not control those and we say so here rather than promise more than we can.

## This file is your private copy. Do not share it.

A **Large** or **Everything** binder contains the full text of documents written by other people
and organisations: appliance manuals, first-aid guides, fire-safety standards, medical-society fact
sheets, university publications. They were fetched for **your personal use** when you downloaded
this file, because in an emergency you need the whole document in your hand, not a web address that
no longer works. A **Small** binder carries only this project's own text and public-domain
material, and names the other documents without including them.

Those documents are copyrighted by their publishers. Giving a Large or Everything binder to someone
else copies their work without permission and breaks copyright. **Keep this file to your own household.** If a
friend wants a binder, send them to the settings page and they can make their own.

## What this binder is, and is not

This binder was assembled and checked by an AI from a verified core text, public datasets and the
answers you gave. **No doctor, engineer, electrician or other professional has reviewed your copy.**
It is a reference for a time when the normal references, services and professionals are not
available. While they are available, use them. Where this binder and a professional disagree, the
professional is right.

Several pages ask you to choose between options that carry real risk. Those pages explain the
hazard, what the manufacturer or agency says and why, what reduces the risk, and the choices. The
choice is yours and so is the responsibility for it.
