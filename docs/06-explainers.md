# Explainers for people new to all of this

Owner decision G4: do not assume the reader knows what kerosene, diesel and gasoline are. Explain
fully but concisely. Each explainer is one page (one phone screen of headings, expandable), written
once, core layer, and placed at the front of its section when `experience = new`, in the appendix
otherwise. The glossary absorbs every term they introduce.
