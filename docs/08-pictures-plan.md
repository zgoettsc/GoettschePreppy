# Pictures, diagrams and the household's own photos

The household binder has 71 public-domain photographs for 25 plant and tree species and no other
figure. For a binder that is the only reference, the wiring, the tourniquet, the knots and the
heater setups are the places where a picture prevents a mistake. The kit fixes this in three
kinds of image, all inline, all offline.

## 1. Public-domain figures (fetch once, ship with the kit)

| Need | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Knots | US Army FM 5-125 plates | PD. 16-03 cites plate numbers today with no plates. |
| Shelters, fire lays, signals, water, snares | Air Force Handbook 10-644 figures | PD. The companion PDF has them; the extraction dropped them. Restore into section 31. |
| Canner, headspace, jar testing, pressure gauge | USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning figures | PD. |
| Wind-chill and heat-index charts | NWS | PD. |
| Safe-room and shelter diagrams | FEMA P-320 | PD. |
| Tourniquet, wound packing, splints, CPR, choking, recovery position | US military medical manuals (TCCC, FM 4-25.11), CDC and NIH illustrations where marked PD | PD only; the Red Cross figures are copyrighted and are fetched per user with their text. |
| Plant and tree plates for the national appendix | USDA PLANTS, ARS, Forest Service, NRCS | PD. Three photographs per species: whole plant, leaf, the diagnostic part (flower, fruit, bark). Owner decision O5: fetch and licence-check once. Target 300 to 400 species. |

Same pipeline as the household binder's plates: licence check per image, caption check against a
reference description, no AI-generated identification imagery ever.

## 2. Line diagrams drawn as SVG (authored once, core)

For the things no agency publishes:

- Series versus parallel panel wiring, and the "never a full string into a small station" rule.
- Generator connection options: extension cords, interlock, transfer switch, cord-and-plug
  conversion; and the backfeed picture with the red line through it.
- The warm room: heater spot, the two vents, the barrier, the alarm at pillow height.
- Portable propane heater with the hose through a window, cylinder outside.
- Gas meter valve: bar in line = on, bar crosswise = off. Water heater drain and cold inlet.
- The two-step calcium hypochlorite method.
- A pool or pot still.
- The two-bucket toilet and the latrine siting distances.
- Fuel can colours and labels.
- The go/no-go decision as a flowchart.

Labelled "diagram, not to scale". Schematics are not identification images, so the AI-image rule
does not apply to them, but each is checked against the page text by the consistency review pass.

## 3. The household's own photographs (attached in the browser, never uploaded)

The highest-value pictures in any binder are of that house. The app has an "add a photo to this
page" button on every page that has a photo slot: the breaker panel with the fridge breaker marked,
the gas meter valve, the water heater valves, the warm room with the heater spot and vents, the
back of each power station, the fuel shelf, the medication shelf, the safe location (not the
combination), the meeting places, the route landmarks. Photos are read by the browser and written
straight into the household's copy. They are not part of the generation request and never leave
the device. The notice says so on the button.

## Priority

Panel wiring and generator connection, then tourniquet and wound packing, then heater setups and
vents, then gas meter, water heater and breaker photo slots, then knots, then CPR and choking, then
the rest. Plates for the national appendix run as a separate long job.
