Pipeline (Python 3 and PIL; no network):

```sh
python3 figures/src/build.py [fig-id ...]   # figures/src/fig_*.py -> figures/svg/*.svg (+ src/meta.json)
python3 figures/src/make_index.py           # -> figures/index.json (SVG meta + PD table + aliases)
python3 figures/src/make_requests.py        # -> figures/REQUESTS.md (scans pages and notes)
```

**How the diagrams are drawn.** Each viewBox is 360 units wide (one phone column). Lines and text
use `currentColor`; neutral shading is `currentColor` at low opacity. The only fixed colours are red
(never / danger), green (correct), blue (water, windows) and amber (heat, labels). The root `<svg>`
carries `color="#1b1b1b"` as a presentation attribute, because `app/binder/styles.js` puts every
`figure.fig svg` on a white card. If A3 ever wants figures to follow the theme, one CSS rule does
it: `figure.fig svg{color:var(--ink);background:none}`. I rendered every diagram in Chromium on
white and on a dark page with a light `color`, and both read.

Every diagram prints "Diagram, not to scale" in its corner. The credit is
"GoettschePreppy (diagram, not to scale)". The SVGs contain no ids (and so no clashes when inlined),
no scripts, no links and no URLs. The `xmlns` attribute is in the files and build-kit strips it.

**Checks.** I checked every diagram against the text of the page it illustrates: numbers, step
order and wording. I looked at a Chromium render of each one and fixed overlaps before accepting
it. I also screenshotted 16-03 and 12-02 inside a generated binder (375 px, dark theme).

## 4. Things for reviewers and other workers

- **MC4 polarity (C08, pages 09-03, 09-12, 09-16).** The pages say the + connector "has the male
  pin". That is not universal: many panel makers put the female coupler on + (the pin genders
  differ by maker). The diagram avoids the claim. It labels "+ lead (as marked on the panel label
  or cable)" and keeps the page's real check, which is that a minus sign means the leads are
  reversed. Suggest C08 soften the sentence to match.
- **Human figures** (CPR adult, child and infant; choking; recovery position) are simple
  schematics, since no PD military figure was obtainable here. Their numbers are copied from the
  12-18 table: 1½ in, about 2 in, 2 to 2.4 in, 100 to 120 a minute, 30:2 and 15:2. A medical
  reviewer should look at the poses. If TC 4-02.1 plates can be fetched later, swap them in.
- **Clearances in fig-propane-heater-hose-route** (30 in above, 24 in front, 6 in each side) are
  from 08-09's Do-this-now list, which itself cites one manufacturer's manual. The diagram says
  "Clearances from one common model: check your label". fig-heater-barrier-clearances leaves them
  blank, as 20-02 asks.
- **fig-generator-connection-options note** "(b) and (c) need a generator whose manual says it
  suits transfer equipment (240 V)" summarises the 09-04 paragraph "Why a transfer switch or
  interlock may not fit your generator".
- **Plant and animal imagery.** I drew none: fig-hemlock-leaf-veins (identification),
  fig-squash-male-female-flowers and fig-tick-removal are planned as PD plates only.
  fig-avma-gunshot-sites-dog-cat is on hold, because the AVMA figures are copyrighted and a drawn
