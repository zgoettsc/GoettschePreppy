## Open questions and choices made

1. **Model names in class pages.** 08-09 and 08-10 take their manufacturer figures from the manuals
   of the Portable Buddy MH9BX and the Dyna-Glo WK11C8. These are the models the owner household
   owns, but they are also very widely sold, and they are the only manuals in the source index.
   Each page names its model as "one widely sold / one common model", never as the household's own
   gear, and points to → 28-04 and the reader's own manual. If you want no model names at all, the
   citations can become "a widely sold indoor propane heater's owner's manual". 30-02 already uses
   that wording.
2. **Numbers dropped because they had no source:**
   - The 158 °F hand-warmer temperature. The pages now say "hot enough to burn", and the owner's
     rule (over clothing only, never in a small child's mittens or boots) is kept as advice with
     its reason.
   - The 68–72 °F pediatric room band.
   - Sleep-sack TOG numbers. The pages now say "follow the label's room chart".
   - Ducted heater fuel use per hour and integral tank size, which came from retail listings.
     There is now a fill-in line instead.
   - The household's indoor-temperature ladder thresholds (64/55/45/40 °F). The ladder is kept,
     with blanks the household fills in.
3. **Numbers kept from the household page with a caveat:** furnace blower "roughly 400 to 800 W"
   ("read your data plate") and a plug-in space heater "typically 1,500 W" ("read both labels").
4. **WK11C8 altitude limit:** I could not confirm one, so 08-10 has a fill-in line. A worker with
   manual access could check.
5. 08-09 no longer lists the hose part numbers. It tells the reader to check the hose tag against
   the manual's accessory list.
6. 11-01's T1D dressing section is now general ("gear under the layers": pumps, sensors, phones,
   fast sugar, epinephrine). It adds condition-specific links when `has.condition.CL-01` or
   `has.epinephrine` is set.
7. The owner's destination-specific wet-cold doctrine is now a general "wet cold near freezing"
   section in 11-01.
8. promote_when for 08-02 includes `has.unvented_heater`, `has.generator` and `has.gas_any` as
   well as `is.cold_winters`. 11-02 adds `has.young_child`, 11-06 adds `is.snow_region`, and 11-09
   uses `has.child`.
