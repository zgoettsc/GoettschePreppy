## How sizes are measured (build-kit)

1. The kit's own copy (`sources/archive/` → `app/build/sources/`; none today).
2. The household binder's archive (`GP_HOUSEHOLD_SOURCES`, default `../SHTF/sources`, using its
   `index.json` `type`/`files`): `archive/<key>.pdf`, `archive/<key>.md`, `manual/<key>.md`, and
   its copy of AFH 10-644 (`companion/afh10-644/afh10-644.pdf`).
3. PDFs are rendered page by page with poppler `pdftoppm` at the same width and quality as pdf.js
   in the browser. The JPEG bytes ×4/3 (base64) give the size. A PDF over 600 pages also keeps the
   PDF itself. Without pdftoppm: pages × 165 KB. Without pdfinfo: the file embedded. Web pages are
   bound as readable text, and the household archive keeps that text as Markdown (size × 1.1).
4. Everything else counts at the measured mean of its kind: a PDF if its address ends in `.pdf`,
   otherwise a web page. Books over 200 pages are left out of the PDF mean (AFH 10-644 would
   triple it). Ranges: measured ×0.95 to ×1.1, averaged ×0.5 to ×2.5.
5. Results are cached in `build/cache/source-sizes.json`. The first build renders about 1,000 pages
   (about 70 s); later builds take seconds.

**Today (600 cited documents, 275 measured):** Small 41 to 55 MB. Large: 381 documents, about 137 MB
before maps, shown as 164 to 371 MB with two maps. Everything: about 461 MB before maps, shown as
438 to 833 MB with two maps. AFH 10-644 alone is about 206 MB (600 page pictures plus the 47 MB PDF).

**Large lands inside 300 to 400 MB without shrinking the pictures**: about 140 MB of pages and
documents plus 54 to 160 MB of maps. So `PDF_RENDER` stays at 1,100 px, quality 0.6.

Legibility test: I rendered the densest pages with pdftoppm at those settings and looked at them:
- FDA EpiPen label, two-column 8 pt: clear.
- Mr. Heater manual p.16, the densest letter page by letter count: clear.
- FDA neffy label p.13, the smallest text in the archive (a 4.6 pt graph legend, about 8 px tall
  at 1,100 px): readable, just. Do not go below 1,100 px.

**Everything is about 0.45 to 0.85 GB, not 1 GB.** The Generate screen shows the measured figure.
The rest of the gap is in the 325 unmeasured documents, about 40 of them PDF manuals that are
probably longer than the 11-page mean.
