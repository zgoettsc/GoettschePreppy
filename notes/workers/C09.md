## Figures requested

- fig-tourniquet-placement: windlass tourniquet 2–3 in above a leg wound, not over the knee; time written on the strap.
- fig-wound-packing: gauze packed into a groin wound with firm hand pressure on top.
- fig-burn-palm-rule: the person's own palm with fingers = about 1% of body surface; adult palm next to a toddler for scale.
- fig-splint-joint-above-below: forearm splint spanning wrist and elbow, padding in gaps, fingers left visible for checks.
- fig-stroke-fast: F.A.S.T. card (face droop, arm drift, speech, time) with a "check blood sugar first" bar above it.
- fig-recovery-position: person on their side, top knee bent forward, head tilted to keep the airway open; adult and child.
- fig-choking-infant: infant face-down on forearm for back blows, then face-up for two-finger chest thrusts.
- fig-choking-child-adult: back blows bent forward, then abdominal thrusts (fist above navel); kneeling version for a small child.
- fig-cpr-infant: two-finger and two-thumb encircling hand positions, about 1½ in depth, mouth over mouth and nose.
- fig-cpr-child: heel of one hand, about 2 in depth, head tilt chin lift.
- fig-cpr-adult: two hands stacked centre of chest, 2–2.4 in depth, 100–120/min.
- fig-closure-strips: clean shallow cut closed with strips laid across, gaps between strips, versus a wound that must stay open.
- fig-evaporative-pouch: pouch soaking, patted dry, worn open to air; crossed-out pouch sealed in a zip bag.
