## Open questions for the coordinator
1. **Tourniquet floor and household 12-02 disagree.** Household 12-02 has a paragraph on
   "tourniquet conversion" (wilderness-medicine practice of loosening once, under conditions,
   when no care will come for hours). Floor 3 says never loosen. I wrote the floor as docs/05
   states it. The 12-02 author must not contradict it. Decide whether docs/05 should say
   anything about conversion as a trained-only skill.
2. **Factual error in household 08-09.** It says a 20-lb cylinder holds "~1,700 cu ft of
   gas". The real figure is about 170 cu ft (20 lb × about 8.5 cu ft per lb). I did not use
   the number. Warn the 08-09 and 28-04 authors.
3. **Camp stoves and grills indoors.** The layer map calls this a floor, but floor 1's wording
   names only "generator, charcoal, or any engine". I covered camp stoves and grills in floor
   1's explanation (citing CDC) and kept them out of DC-20. DC-20 covers candles, oil lamps,
   and gas-range or hearth cooking. Consider adding camp stoves to the docs/05 floor 1 wording.
4. **DC-10 `humane-end` points to a method on → 20-09.** The 20-09 author needs to include a
   method section based on the AVMA guidelines, or the card points at nothing.
5. **DC-16 `last-resort` mentions "a published adult table printed as published".** The
   household 12-04 prints no such table; it declines to. The 12-04 author or coordinator must
   decide whether one exists. If not, the clinician's written plan is the only source the
   option names.
6. **How I read the DC-13 options.**
   - `distill-only`: pool, hot-tub and surface water are drunk only distilled.
   - `filter-and-chlorinate`: allowed only for freshwater pools with known additives.
   - `drink-treated-surface-water`: the full treatment ladder for streams and ponds.
   - Salt pools always need distilling. That is a fact, not a choice.
7. **How I read DC-23.** CDC and EPA already double the dose for cloudy water. I read
   `double-dose-and-wait` as "skip clearing the water and rely on a heavy dose and a longer
   wait".
8. **DC-09 `bed-share-with-rules` is offered for children over 12 months only.** For a baby
   under 12 months the card says `separate-sleep-surface` is the only option (AAP).
9. **Manufacturer manuals are cited by publisher in the text.** These are Enerco/Mr. Heater,
   GHP/Dyna-Glo and YSN/Flame King. They are the owner household's gear brands, used as
   "one widely sold heater" examples, and model names are not in the body. Change them to
   generic wording if you prefer.
10. **Region-hint ids in `derived_from`.** I dropped `02-06-hugh-anaphylaxis-action-plan`
    from `derived_from` because its id holds an owner name. Two others carry region hints but
    are kept because the page plan uses them: `14-11-defense-and-sd-law` and
    `05-06-foraging-northern-plains`.
