Each class page has three parts: "What matters and why", a model table, and an "Our gear" table.
Every number in the model tables comes from the maker's own manual or spec page, and the maker is
named in the row. A cell reads "see label" (or "not stated" on the filter page) when the document
did not publish that figure. Floors on 28-03 and 28-04 are worded as in 30-01. Heater indoor use
points to DC-01 and DC-02 (→ 30-02, → 30-03). Generator connection points to the floors (→ 30-01)
and DC-07 (→ 30-08).

The propane figures on 28-04 are sourced. A full 20 lb is about 170 cu ft of gas (Matheson gives
8.62 cu ft/lb at 70 °F), and the page says "170, not 1,700". NIST says most 20-lb cylinders are
filled to 15 lb, so about 130 cu ft. Gallons and BTU are left to 06-05.

## Open questions
- **MH9BX and WK11C8 rows** (see above).
- **Contradictory solar-input ceilings:** the Anker F3800 is 11–60 V in Anker's user guide, while
  the current US product page (F3800 Plus) is 11–165 V. Both are shown as separate rows because
  the contrast is the lesson. Confirm that the US F3800 (non-Plus) is still 60 V.
- **The household's "current limit is harmless" rule was softened.** Victron and EcoFlow both
  publish a maximum PV short-circuit current whose excess may damage the unit, so 28-02 says so.
