## Open questions and judgement calls

1. **Fuel shelf lives.** No federal or manufacturer source found gives a number for gasoline,
   diesel or kerosene storage life (the household's "about 6 months" and "2 to 5 years sealed" are
   unsourced planning figures). EX-01 gives no number: it says gasoline keeps "months, not years",
   with Honda's stabiliser facts, and points to DC-17 for kerosene and diesel. If a source is
   found (EIA has none), a number can be added.
2. **Owner gear removed from citations.** The household's kerosene-heater manual (GHP/Dyna-Glo
   WK11C8) carries the "blue can only" and "not one season to the next" lines, but citing it would
   name the owner's model, so EX-01 does not cite it and keeps only the general convention.
3. **Planning ranges kept from verified household pages without a federal source:** appliance
   loads in EX-02 (phone 10 to 20 Wh, kettle 1,200 to 1,800 W, fridge 100 to 200 W / 1,000 to 2,000
   Wh a day; household 09-01 and 25-02 "typical, check your own"), and radio ranges in EX-09
   (household 15-02). Both are labelled as typical, with an instruction to measure your own.
   Cal-hypo strength 65 to 73% is from household 03-04 (product labels).
4. **Alert names.** EX-10 uses "extreme cold warning" and "extreme heat warning" and says older
   radios may say "wind chill warning" or "excessive heat warning". The household confirmed the
   heat renaming only from local reports; check both renamings against NWS when the region data is
   built.
5. **EX-12** carries standard USFA advice that is not on the two USFA pages I added (never water on
   a grease fire, stop-drop-roll, close doors behind you, two ways out). A USFA cooking-fire or
   escape-plan page could be added as a source.
6. **EX-15** says Large includes topo maps (G16) and that Small should also stay within the
   household because it holds private answers (privacy advice, not copyright). The owner may want
   different wording for Small.
7. EX-01 is the longest page (about 1,500 words including two tables) because the owner asked for
   the full fuels table; the others are 680 to 1,100 words.
