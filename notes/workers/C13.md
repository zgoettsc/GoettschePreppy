## How the age bands work

Section 20 pages print a "Your children" line (`#each children`, `#each teens`, `#each infants`), a
"Read first for your household" list driven by flags, then full sections for every band in age
order (infant, toddler, preschool, school-age, teen). Every band is always present; flags only
tailor the top-of-page pointers and wording. The owner's 4-year-old / 15-month-old specifics are
gone; per-child items are fill-ins.

## DC-10 humane-end method (20-09) — please read
Written from the AVMA 2020 text itself (avma.org blocks fetchers; read a university-hosted copy of the
same PDF): S1.2 (IV barbiturate preferred; DEA-registered), S1.3/S1.5 gunshot paragraph ("acceptable
with conditions", only highly skilled firearm-trained personnel, legal jurisdictions, remote areas or
emergencies with prolonged unrelieved suffering or imminent danger to human life, not routine, sedation
recommended for cats), unacceptable list (drowning, hypothermia, household chemicals, disinfectants,
cleaning agents, pesticides; injection routes), M3.5.4 firearm safety and muzzle 1-2 ft / never flush,
I7 confirmation of death, I8 disposal, Figures 8-9 captions. Note honestly on the page: the AVMA does
**not** list any method as acceptable *for owners* as such; gunshot is limited to skilled firearm users,
so the page tells a household without that skill to find someone who has it. Vet-first statement and a
pre-collapse law callout are at the top. Other species (rabbits, birds, reptiles, fish) are pointed to
the vet and the bound AVMA document, not printed. The owner may want to review this section.
