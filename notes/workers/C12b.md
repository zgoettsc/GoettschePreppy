- **Shelter by dwelling with no basement, no named room, not apartment, not mobile home**: written as nested `{{#if !…}}`. A `has.single_family` or `dwelling.storeys` flag would allow cleaner wording.
- **Heater burn rate / generator gal per hour**: no `gear.*` values; 23-02 says "from the label" with blanks.
