# Worker brief (content workers)

You are re-authoring one family's verified SHTF binder into GoettschePreppy, a kit that generates an
offline survival reference for any US household. Your pages ship to strangers who are new to
prepping. Read this brief, then the documents it names, then write your pages.

## Paths
- Kit repo (write here): `/home/user/goettschepreppy`
- Household binder (READ ONLY, never modify): `/home/user/SHTF`. Pages are under
  `sections/NN-*/NN-MM-slug.md`. They were fact-checked and fixed on 2026-11-02, so their facts are
  the best available; the owner household's personal details and risk choices are what you remove.

## Read first (all of them)
1. `docs/00-decisions.md` (owner decisions; binding)
2. `docs/09-authoring-spec.md` (THE contract: page format, template syntax, conventions)
3. `docs/05-decision-cards.md` (cards DC-01..DC-24 and the floors; use these ids)
4. `docs/06-explainers.md` (EX-01..EX-15; point novices at them)
5. `schema/context.json` (every slot path, flag and list you may use; nothing else exists)
6. `data/page-plan.json` (every kit page id; use it for cross-references)
7. `docs/04-condition-library.md` if you touch medicine.

## Rules that matter most
- **Write only your assigned pages**, plus `notes/workers/<your-id>.md` and
  `sources/additions/<your-id>.json`. Do not edit any other file. Do not run git.
- **Never a URL** anywhere, including frontmatter. Sources are keys from `sources/index.json`.
  To find the key for a household page's source, match its URL against the `url` field in
  `sources/index.json`. New sources go in your additions file with all fields (`key`, `title`,
  `org`, `date`, `licence_class`, `licence_note`, `url`, `household_cited_by: []`, `cited_by: []`).
  Only add a source you are confident exists with that title and publisher.
- **Nothing from the owner household**: names, towns, destinations, phones, their gear models,
  their house layout, their decided rules. `node scripts/validate.mjs` fails on the token list in
  `scripts/owner-tokens.json`, but the list is not complete; you are responsible for the rest.
  Replace with slots (`{{ home.county }}`, `{{#each members as m}}`) or general wording.
- **Owner risk choices are never defaults.** Where the household page prints a "household rule"
  that is a trade-off, write the trade-off neutrally in one or two sentences, insert
  `{{choice DC-NN}}` and `→ 30-NN` (page 30-(NN+1) holds card DC-NN; e.g. DC-01 is → 30-02).
- **Floors** (docs/05 part B) are stated as rules with the reason, worded as in docs/05.
- **Novice level.** Explain every term on first use or point to an explainer (→ 29-NN). Short
  sentences. Concrete. Keep "Do this now" lists at the top of emergency pages.
- **Everything is always included.** `#if` tailors wording; it never removes a topic.
- **No derived dosing.** Published tables printed as published, publisher named.
- **Keep the verified facts.** Do not drop numbers, warnings or procedures that are true for any
  household; do not introduce new numbers without a source.
- **Cross-references** `→ NN-MM` using kit ids from the page plan. Household refs to Section 02 map
  to the condition library (02-01/02-02 T1D, 02-03 anaphylaxis, 02-04 food allergy); 02-09 → 20-09;
  02-10 → 12-21; 02-12 → 01-07. Refs to household 28/29/30, 02-11, 05-20 are removed or redirected.
- Frontmatter: `version: 1`, `revised: 2026-11-16`, `review_pass: null`, `derived_from` = the
  household ids you used (or []), `layer` per the page plan, `danger_level` kept from the household
  page unless the generalised page clearly changes it.
- Section intros (`NN-00-section-intro`) are generated by script. Do not write them.
- Length: about the household page's length minus the owner detail. Do not pad.

## Done means
- Every assigned page exists and `node scripts/validate.mjs <your section folder>` shows 0 errors
  for your pages (warnings about related pages not yet written are fine).
- `notes/workers/<your-id>.md` lists: pages written; slot paths, flags or lists you needed but
  could not find (and what you used instead); figures requested (`fig-id: what it must show`);
  photo slots added; sources added; open questions; anything not finished.
- Your final message to the coordinator: 5 to 10 lines, the counts and anything blocking. Do not
  paste page content.
